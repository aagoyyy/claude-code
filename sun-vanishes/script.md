# What If the Sun Vanished? — Minute by Minute

**Format:** long-form, 16:9, ~9:30 runtime, English VO, ~150 wpm
**Gaya:** timeline countdown. Timer `T+ 00:00` selalu ada di pojok layar, dan tiap bab diawali timestamp baru.
**Tone:** tenang, sinematik, rasa ingin tahu. Bukan clickbait panik.

**Judul (pilih satu, A/B test nanti):**
1. What If the Sun Vanished? (Minute by Minute)
2. The Last 8 Minutes of Sunlight
3. If the Sun Disappeared Right Now, Would You Notice?

**Thumbnail:** Bumi kecil berpendar di kiri, area hitam besar di kanan tempat Matahari seharusnya ada, teks `8:20`.

---

## 0. COLD OPEN (0:00 – 0:40)

**VO:**
Right now, sunlight is touching your skin.

That light left the Sun eight minutes and twenty seconds ago.
Which means the Sun you're seeing... is already the past.

So here's a question. If something erased the Sun — right now, this very second —
would you notice?

Would the Earth?

Let's find out. Minute by minute.

**VISUAL:** Layar hitam. Satu garis cahaya menyentuh Bumi. Cut ke wide shot Matahari dan Bumi. Timer muncul: `T+ 00:00`.

---

## 1. T+ 00:00 — "Nothing Happens" (0:40 – 2:10)

**VO:**
Quick disclaimer: this can't actually happen. A star can't just blink out of existence.
But as a thought experiment, it reveals something beautiful about how our universe works.

So. The Sun is gone. Zero seconds.

Look up. It's still there. Still bright. Still warm.
Your shadow hasn't moved an inch.

Because the last light the Sun ever made is still on its way.
Light travels at about three hundred thousand kilometers every second,
and the Sun is a hundred and fifty million kilometers away.
That trip takes eight minutes and twenty seconds.

But here's the part that surprises most people.

Gravity travels at the same speed.

Einstein's theory says that a change in gravity can't arrive instantly.
It spreads outward at the speed of light — and so does the news.

So for the next eight minutes, the Earth has no idea.
It keeps curving around a Sun that isn't there,
following the memory of a pull that no longer exists.

**VISUAL:** Matahari lenyap (flash singkat). Cincin cahaya mengembang dari titik Matahari menuju Bumi. Bumi tetap mengorbit. Timer jalan `00:00 → 08:20`. Kamera dolly pelan mendekati Bumi.

---

## 2. T+ 08:20 — "The Switch" (2:10 – 3:50)

**VO:**
Eight minutes. Twenty seconds.

The last wave of light reaches Earth.
And in the very same instant, the Sun's gravity lets go.

On the day side, the sky goes dark.
Not like a sunset. No glow on the horizon. No twilight.
Just a switch, flipped.

The Moon vanishes with it. It never made its own light — it was only ever reflecting the Sun.
The same goes for Venus, Mars, and Jupiter.
All that's left in the sky... are the stars.

And the Earth stops orbiting.

For the first time in four and a half billion years, nothing is pulling it into a curve.
Newton's first law finally gets to do its job:
Earth flies off in a straight line, at about thirty kilometers per second.
Forever.

**VISUAL:** Cincin cahaya sampai ke Bumi. Sisi terang Bumi padam dalam sekejap. Bulan ikut gelap. Bumi lepas dari garis orbit (garis orbit putus-putus tertinggal di belakang). Lampu kota di sisi malam tetap menyala.

---

## 3. T+ 1 HOUR to 1 DAY — "The Slow Cold" (3:50 – 5:00)

**VO:**
You'd expect everything to freeze instantly. It doesn't.

Our atmosphere and oceans hold an enormous amount of heat.
For the first few hours, it's almost normal outside. Just... dark.

Streetlights stay on. Most power grids keep running.
Your phone still works.

But the quiet damage has already begun.
The tiny plankton that make up the base of the ocean food chain —
they run on sunlight. No light, no food.

And the weather? Weather is an engine, and the Sun was its fuel.
Without it, winds and clouds slowly wind down.

**VISUAL:** Kota malam dari ketinggian. Timer lompat `T+ 01:00:00`, lalu `T+ 24:00:00`. Termometer global turun pelan. Kamera masuk ke lautan, plankton meredup.

---

## 4. T+ 1 WEEK to 1 YEAR — "Below Zero" (5:00 – 6:20)

**VO:**
After one week, the average temperature at the surface has fallen below freezing.

After a year, it's somewhere around minus seventy degrees Celsius.

The oceans begin to freeze, from the top down.
That ice acts like a lid, sealing in whatever warmth is left below.

Plants stop growing. Most die within weeks.
A few big trees might hold on a while longer on stored energy,
but with nothing to eat them, nothing is eating anything.

The food chain doesn't break all at once.
It unravels, link by link.

**VISUAL:** Time-lapse global: biru menjadi putih. Peta suhu mengalir turun. Pohon menggugurkan daun lalu membeku. Timer: `T+ 7 DAYS`, `T+ 1 YEAR`.

---

## 5. "Could Anyone Survive?" (6:20 – 7:50)

**VO:**
Not on the surface. But maybe not nowhere.

The Earth has its own heat, buried deep inside.
Iceland already warms most of its homes with it.

A small group of people, living underground near geothermal vents,
growing food under artificial lights, could last a long time.

It wouldn't be a life. It would be survival.
Long, dark, and very, very cold.

And all the while, the planet carries them further from home —
out of the solar system, into the dark between the stars.

**VISUAL:** Potongan penampang tanah: kota bawah tanah kecil, lampu tanam, uap geotermal. Kamera mundur keluar dari Bumi ke ruang hampa.

---

## 6. "The Wanderer" (7:50 – 9:10)

**VO:**
Earth is now a rogue planet, drifting at thirty kilometers per second.

The nearest star, Proxima Centauri, is just over four light-years away.
At this speed, crossing that distance would take about forty-two thousand years —
and that's if Earth were even pointed that way.
It isn't.

Meanwhile, the news keeps spreading.

Mercury finds out in three minutes.
Jupiter, forty-three minutes after the Sun vanished.
Neptune, about four hours.

One by one, each planet loses its anchor and drifts away.
A wave of silence, rolling out through the solar system
at the speed of light.

**VISUAL:** Gelombang cincin cahaya melewati tiap planet. Planet meluncur lurus ke luar satu per satu. Kamera ke atas (top-down): tata surya yang bubar seperti kembang api yang pelan.

---

## 7. CLOSING (9:10 – 9:40)

**VO:**
The Sun is still there today.

Right now, light that left it eight minutes ago is warming your face.

Everything you see is a little bit in the past.
And for eight minutes and twenty seconds, even the end of the world
would look exactly like a normal day.

Next time: what happens if the Moon disappears?

**VISUAL:** Kembali ke shot pembuka. Matahari menyala utuh. Fade ke hitam. End card.
