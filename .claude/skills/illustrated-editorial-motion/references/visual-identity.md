# Visual identity — Illustrated Editorial Explainer Motion Graphics

**Feel:** a premium editorial explainer, not a corporate slide deck. Warm, tactile, playful but credible; every frame has one clear subject and one clear number or message.

## Pillars
1. **Paper world** — warm ivory/cream paper, faint graph grid, soft grain. Objects sit *on* the paper or on a sand-colored table.
2. **Ink outline** — one warm near-black outline (`derived.outline`, 5 viewBox-units) on every object; slight organic wobble (`ink-rough` filter).
3. **Flat + one shade** — flat fills, one soft shadow shape, one white highlight stroke. No gradients on objects.
4. **Numbers as heroes** — prices, percentages and costs are large, high-contrast, animated.
5. **Story objects drive motion** — an object, not a generic effect, moves the story (a price tag sweeps the screen as a transition).

## Avoid
PowerPoint-style fly-ins, stock vector mixes, neon, gradients on objects, 3D renders, decorative clutter, bounce > one overshoot, random motion, scenes that change illustration style.

## Scene vocabulary (composition patterns)
isolated object · top-down · illustrated environment · character-centered · infographic · comparison · sequential assembly · before/after · visual metaphor.

## Layers (z-order)
1. Background (paper / environment / surface)  2. Ground shadow  3. Hero object  4. Supporting objects  5. Labels / price tags  6. Sparkles  7. Fixed typography overlay  8. Transition overlay.
